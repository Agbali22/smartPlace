SmartPlace v4.0

Supported sensors: BH1750 (light), BME280/DHT22 (temp+humidity). Extra JSON fields are accepted and ignored.

SmartPlace

A spot-checking tool that tells you whether a specific location is right for what you want to place there. Put the sensor in the exact spot, select your object, and get a live score with specific advice.

Context Inputs
